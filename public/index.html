Privacy Policy

Saeed BinShafar Properties Mobile Application

Application: Saeed BinShafar Properties (iOS: com.eliteliving.app | Android: com.binshafar.properties) Managed by: Saeed BinShafar Properties / Elite Living Real Estate Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Saeed BinShafar Properties ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Saeed BinShafar Properties mobile application (the "App") and related property management services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide property management, tenancy, and maintenance services:

a. Personal & Account Information

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Property & Unit Details: Building name/identifier, apartment or unit number, and user role (tenant, technician, or property administrator).
  • Account Credentials: Authentication identifiers managed securely via Firebase Authentication.

b. Maintenance Requests & Media

  • Service Requests & Communications: Descriptions of maintenance issues, status updates, and chat messages exchanged with property management or assigned technicians.
  • Photos & Camera Access: When you choose to attach photos to a maintenance request, the App may access your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) or allow you to select photos through your device's system photo picker. Photos are uploaded solely to document and resolve the reported maintenance issue.

c. Tenancy & Contract Information

  • Lease & Payment Records: Lease contract dates, unit assignments, and rent cheque/payment status records displayed to authorized tenants and property administrators.

d. Device & Notification Data

  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens are collected and stored strictly to deliver timely notifications regarding maintenance status updates, technician assignments, chat replies, and tenancy alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to operate and support our property management services:

  • To authenticate your account and verify your residency or staff authorization.
  • To submit, track, assign, and resolve building and apartment maintenance requests.
  • To display lease contract details and cheque/payment schedules to authorized users.
  • To facilitate communication between tenants, maintenance technicians, and property management.
  • To send service-related push notifications about maintenance progress and tenancy updates.
  • To maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, and we do not share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Your information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Property Management Personnel & Technicians: Maintenance requests, unit numbers, contact phone numbers, and uploaded issue photos are accessible to authorized Saeed BinShafar Properties administrators and assigned maintenance technicians solely to coordinate and complete requested repairs.
  • Google Firebase (Infrastructure & Cloud Services): We use Google Firebase services—specifically Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions for Firebase, and Firebase Cloud Messaging—to securely authenticate users, store application data, host maintenance photos, and deliver push notifications. Google processes this data on our behalf in accordance with Google Cloud / Firebase privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process.

4. Data Security & Retention

  • Security Measures: All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase servers protected by strict role-based access rules and security controls.
  • Retention: We retain your account and tenancy records for as long as your account or tenancy remains active, or as necessary to fulfill property management, contractual, and legal obligations.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information:

  • Update Profile: You can update your name and phone number directly within the App under Profile → Edit Profile.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and associated personal profile data directly inside the App at any time by going to Profile → Delete Account.
  • Deletion by Request: You may also request account or data deletion by contacting us using the details below or by viewing our Account Deletion Instructions.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult tenants, property administrators, and maintenance staff and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
