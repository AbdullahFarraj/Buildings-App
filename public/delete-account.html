Account & Data Deletion

Saeed BinShafar Properties (com.eliteliving.app)

Users of the Saeed BinShafar Properties mobile application can delete their account and associated personal data directly in the app or by submitting a deletion request to property management.

Option 1: Delete Your Account Directly in the App

  1. Open the Saeed BinShafar Properties app and sign in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm the prompt to permanently delete your authentication account and user profile.

Note: For security reasons, if you have not signed in recently, the app may ask you to sign out and sign back in before completing the deletion.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email or Phone

If you no longer have access to the app or would like us to delete your account and associated records on your behalf, please contact us with your registered full name, email address, phone number, and apartment number:

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication account, push notification tokens, and personal user profile record (name, email, phone number).
  • Retained where required: Historical building maintenance logs and financial/lease records required by applicable property management or legal obligations may be retained in anonymized or archival form.
